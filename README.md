# Dual Admit

Landing page for **Dual Admit** — a college admissions counseling service founded by Krishna Kasuganti and Sai Maruvada, helping high school students build competitive applications and get into their dream schools.

**Live site:** [dualadmit.netlify.app](https://dualadmit.netlify.app)

---

## About

Dual Admit was built on a simple idea: the best college admissions advice comes from people who just went through it. Krishna got into CS + Math at Duke (5% acceptance rate) and Sai got into McCombs at UT Austin (3% acceptance rate). Together they offer personalized counseling — essays, strategy, interview prep, and more — to students who want real guidance from people who've been there.

---

## What's on the Page

- **Hero** — Headline and booking CTA with an interactive grid reveal on mouse movement
- **Stats strip** — Key credentials (acceptance rates, national rankings)
- **Counselor profiles** — Individual sections for Krishna and Sai with photo grids and their admissions stories
- **Testimonials** — Auto-scrolling cards from past students across UNC, Emory, NC State, and more
- **Services** — Application strategy, essay coaching, and interview preparation
- **FAQ** — Collapsible accordion answering common questions
- **Footer** — Navigation, booking links, and social

---

## Tech Stack

- **[Tailwind CSS v4](https://tailwindcss.com)** — Utility-first CSS, compiled locally to a static stylesheet
- **Google Fonts** — Inter typeface
- **Vanilla JavaScript** — Mouse tracking for the hero grid reveal effect
- **Netlify** — Hosting with automatic deploys from GitHub
