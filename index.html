We're here to help you

What's your typical project timeline?

Timelines vary based on your goals and where you are in the process. Some students work with us over several months, while others book sessions for specific parts of their application.

Do you offer revisions?

Yes. We work with you iteratively especially on essays. Great writing takes multiple passes, and we're with you through every round until it's right.

What do you need from me to get started?

Just book a free call, and we'll take care of the rest. No prep required! We'll walk you through everything during the intro session.

Can you work with my existing materials?

Absolutely. If you have draft essays, a resume, or a school list already started, we work from where you are whether that is early planning or last-minute polish.

What makes Dual Admit different?

We're not career counselors. We're recent students who went through the exact same process and got into schools like Duke and UT Austin. We know what works because we lived it.

Do you provide support beyond essays?

Yes! Scholarship applications, honors college essays, pitch competition prep, time management, and full application strategy. Essays are just one piece of the puzzle.
